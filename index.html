04 / HOW I WORKTHE LOOP IS THE PRODUCT

A repeatable instinct

Make the hard
parts feel lighter.

Good software is not a pile of features. It is a sequence of decisions that makes the important thing easier to see, choose, and trust.

My default loopObserve → model → build → verify
01

Observe

Find the real constraint, not just the loudest request. Trace the handoffs, the wait states, and the places people make workarounds.

LISTEN
02

Model

Turn ambiguity into a map: roles, data, edge cases, states, and the smallest useful shape of the product.

CLARIFY
03

Build

Make the system legible from the inside out: stable APIs, clear components, and interfaces with a point of view.

CRAFT
04

Verify

Cross-check the output. Test the flow. Watch the details. Ship something that earns confidence in the real world.
